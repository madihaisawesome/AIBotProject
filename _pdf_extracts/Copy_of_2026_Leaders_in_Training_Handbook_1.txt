LEADERSHIP BEGINS HERE.
Congratulations, and welcome to one of Ambition Performing Arts’ most distinguished 
educational programs.
The Leaders in Training (LIT) Program is designed to help students grow into confident 
leaders through meaningful service, practical leadership experience, and intentional 
leadership education. Throughout your journey, you’ll develop skills that extend far 
beyond the studio, preparing you to make a positive impact in your school, community, 
future career, and wherever life takes you.
Leadership isn’t something you’re given.
It’s something you build through consistent choices, a willingness to learn, and a 
commitment to serving others.
Whether you’re beginning as an Assistant or continuing your leadership journey as an 
Apprentice, every opportunity is designed to help you grow with confidence, integrity, 
and purpose.
We’re excited to watch you learn, grow, lead, and discover what you’re capable of.
Welcome to the journey.

OUR MISSION  
& VISION
As a Leaders in Training student, you’re part of 
something bigger than a classroom. Every leader 
at APA, from our Assistants to our Directors, is 
guided by the same mission and vision. These 
aren’t just words on our walls; they shape the way 
we teach, serve, communicate, and lead every day.
OUR MISSION
Raising tomorrow’s leaders through the arts.
OUR VISION
We create safe, welcoming spaces where children 
feel they belong, grow in confidence, and discover 
who they are through the arts.
 
 
 
WHAT YOU  
CAN EXPECT
The Leaders in Training Program is about MORE 
than classroom leadership. Every opportunity is 
intentionally designed to help students develop 
confidence, character, and leadership while making 
a positive impact on those around them.
Throughout the program, we will:
• Instill a FAMILY FIRST attitude by centering our 
program around the pillars of Trust, Serving, 
Love, Excellence, and Growth.
• Teach students to become POSITIVE ROLE 
MODELS through practical classroom 
experience, instructor mentoring, and 
intentional leadership learning focused on 
gratitude, humility, empathy, initiative, and a 
growth mindset.
• Build SELF CONFIDENCE by providing a safe 
learning environment where students take 
responsibility, embrace accountability, and 
discover what they are capable of.
• Provide opportunities to GIVE BACK TO THEIR 
COMMUNITY by sharing a love of the arts with 
younger generations while creating a lasting 
impact within our studio and community.
YOUR LEADERSHIP JOURNEY
ASSISTANT LEADER APPRENTICE LEADERS 
IN ACTION 
Leadership is developed one experience at a time.
As students grow in confidence, responsibility, and 
leadership, they’ll have the opportunity to progress 
through four stages of the Leaders in Training 
Program. Each stage is designed to provide age-
appropriate opportunities while preparing students 
for the next step in their leadership journey.
Progression is based on readiness, coachability, 
commitment, and a desire to continue growing, not 
simply age or years in the program.
ASSISTANT
Learning the Foundations
The Assistant level introduces students to 
leadership through observation, service, and 
guided mentorship. Students begin learning 
what it means to represent APA as they develop 
confidence in the classroom.
To apply, students must:
• Be registered in at least one performing arts 
class at Ambition Performing Arts for the 
current season.
• Be entering Grade 5 or higher and be 10 years 
of age by September 1.
• Demonstrate Pre-Intermediate level skills or 
receive a Teacher Recommendation.
• Demonstrate enthusiasm for learning, serving, 
and personal growth.
• Successfully complete the Leaders in Training 
application and interview process.

LEADER
Growing as a Leader
As Leaders, students take on greater 
responsibility while continuing to strengthen their 
communication, professionalism, and classroom 
leadership skills. This level encourages students to 
lead by example while supporting younger dancers 
with confidence and care.
To apply, students must:
• Be registered in at least one performing arts 
class at Ambition Performing Arts for the 
current season.
• Be entering Grade 7 or higher and be 12 years 
of age by September 1.
• Successfully complete the Assistant level (or 
demonstrate equivalent experience).
• Demonstrate enthusiasm for learning, serving, 
and personal growth.
• Successfully complete the Leaders in Training 
application and interview process.
APPRENTICE
Preparing for the Profession
The Apprentice level is designed for students who 
are ready to take the next step in their leadership 
journey. Apprentices receive advanced leadership 
opportunities while developing the professionalism, 
communication, and teaching skills expected of 
future educators.
To apply, students must:
• Be registered in at least one performing arts 
class at Ambition Performing Arts for the 
current season.
• Be entering Grade 9 or higher and be 14 years 
of age by September 1.
• Successfully complete previous Leaders in 
Training levels (or demonstrate equivalent 
experience).
• Demonstrate enthusiasm for learning, serving, 
and personal growth.
• Successfully complete the Leaders in Training 
application and interview process.
LEADERS IN ACTION
Preparing Future Educators
For students who see teaching as part of their 
future, Leaders in Action bridges the gap between 
leadership and professional education. 
Unlike the Leaders in Training Program, Leaders 
in Action is a registered leadership class that 
combines advanced leadership education with 
practical teaching experience. Students continue to 
develop their skills through a structured curriculum 
while applying their learning through meaningful 
leadership opportunities throughout the studio.
Leaders in Action is designed for students who are 
committed to exploring teaching professionally 
and preparing for future employment opportunities 
within the performing arts.
To apply, students must:
• Be registered in at least one performing arts 
class at Ambition Performing Arts for the 
current season.
• Be entering Grade 11 or higher and be 16 years 
of age by September 1.
• Successfully complete previous Leaders in 
Training levels (or demonstrate equivalent 
experience).
• Demonstrate enthusiasm for learning, serving, 
and personal growth.
• Successfully complete the Leaders in Action 
application and selection process.
Participation in Leaders in Action is by invitation 
and registration only. Students are selected based 
on their leadership, professionalism, coachability, 
readiness for advanced training, and the needs of 
the program.

interested in exploring teaching as a future career. 
Unlike the Leaders in Training Program, which

class that prepares students for the art and 
profession of teaching.
Students will continue assisting in classrooms while 
participating in a 30-minute weekly leadership 
class focused on developing the knowledge, skills, 
and confidence needed to become exceptional 
educators.

topics such as:

HOW WE  
DEVELOP LEADERS
Great leaders aren’t developed by reading about 
leadership. They’re developed through education, 
experience, reflection, and service.
The Leaders in Training Program combines 
leadership education, practical leadership 
experience, mentorship, and ongoing feedback 
to help students develop confidence, character, 
and real-world leadership skills. Every opportunity 
is intentionally designed to help students grow 
as confident leaders, both inside and outside the 
studio.
Leaders in Training students participate in four key 
areas of learning.
LEADERSHIP EDUCATION
Each month, students participate in a leadership 
development session using our STAR Leadership 
curriculum. These interactive lessons explore 
topics that help students become confident, 
compassionate, and capable leaders.

Students receive classroom observations and 
feedback from the instructors they work alongside, 
helping them recognize their progress and 
celebrate milestones throughout the year.

LEADERSHIP  
STANDARDS
Leadership isn’t defined by a title. 
It’s reflected in the choices we make every day.
Every Leader in Training student represents 
Ambition Performing Arts through their words, 
actions, attitude, and example. These standards 
guide the way we learn, lead, and serve together.
I Lead By Example
I understand that younger students learn by 
watching me. I choose to model respect, kindness, 
responsibility, and a positive attitude both inside 
and outside the studio.
I Am Reliable
I arrive prepared, on time, and ready to contribute. 
I understand that my teachers and students are 
counting on me.
I Choose Integrity
I make honest choices, follow through on my 
commitments, and do the right thing even when 
no one is watching. I understand that trust is 
earned through consistency, accountability, and 
integrity.
I Serve Others
Leadership is about serving, not being in charge. 
I look for ways to help, encourage, and support 
those around me without being asked.
I Am Coachable
I welcome feedback with a positive attitude and 
understand that growth comes through learning, 
reflection, and practice.
I Communicate Professionally
I communicate respectfully with students, families, 
instructors, and fellow leaders. When I have 
questions or concerns, I speak directly with my 
instructor or program leader.
I Protect Our Studio Culture
I help create an environment where every student 
feels safe, welcomed, included, and encouraged to 
grow.
I Continue Learning
Leadership is a lifelong journey. I embrace 
challenges, learn from mistakes, celebrate progress, 
and continually look for opportunities to grow.

CLASSROOM 
LEADERSHIP
Every classroom is different, but the goal is always 
the same: create a positive learning environment 
where students feel safe, supported, and 
encouraged to grow.
As a Leaders in Training student, your role is to 
support the instructor, encourage students, and 
help create a positive classroom experience. 
You are never expected to replace the instructor 
but instead work alongside them as part of the 
teaching team.
Your greatest contribution isn’t doing more than 
everyone else. It’s helping your instructor and 
students have a better experience because you 
were there.
BEFORE CLASS
• Arrive early and prepared.
• Review your classroom assignment.
• Help prepare equipment and learning materials.
• Welcome students as they arrive.
• Connect with your instructor about the lesson 
plan or any classroom needs.
• Be ready to notice what your instructor needs 
before they have to ask.
DURING CLASS
• Support the instructor’s teaching rather than 
taking over the lesson.
• Lead by example.
• Encourage participation.
• Assist students when directed.
• Help maintain a safe classroom environment.
• Stay engaged and avoid side conversations.
AFTER CLASS
• Help tidy the classroom.
• Assist with equipment and props.
• Answer questions within your role and refer 
more complex questions to your instructor or 
the office team.
• Check in with your instructor before leaving.
• Take a moment to reflect on what went well 
and one way you can continue growing.
Leadership is about serving, not being in charge.
At APA, leadership isn’t measured by how much 
you do - it’s measured by how well you help 
others succeed. 
PROFESSIONAL 
EXPECTATIONS
Professionalism isn’t about being perfect. It’s about 
showing respect for your students, your team, and 
the responsibility you’ve been entrusted with.
As a Leaders in Training student, you are an 
ambassador for Ambition Performing Arts. The 
habits you develop now will serve you throughout 
your leadership journey and in future workplaces, 
schools, and communities.
REPRESENTING APA
Whether I am in the studio, at a competition, 
community event, or online, I understand that my 
actions reflect the values of Ambition Performing 
Arts.
I will represent APA with professionalism, kindness, 
integrity, and respect in everything I do.
ATTENDANCE & COMMITMENT
Students, families, and instructors rely on our 
Leaders in Training team to help create a positive 
classroom experience. As a Leader in Training 
student, I will:
• Attend my assigned classes whenever possible.
• Notify the Leadership Coordinator as early as 
possible if I am unable to attend.
• Arrive on time and prepared for each 
assignment.
• Understand that consistent attendance 
demonstrates reliability and respect for my 
team.
PROFESSIONAL PRESENTATION
As a leader, I understand that my presentation sets 
an example for younger students. I will:
• Follow the Leaders in Training dress code.
• Wear appropriate footwear and attire for the 
classroom.
• Maintain good personal hygiene.
• Present myself in a neat, professional manner.
CELL PHONES & TECHNOLOGY
My attention belongs with my students. I will:
• Keep my phone put away during leadership 
assignments unless directed by an instructor.
• Use technology only for approved leadership 
purposes.
• Stay fully engaged with students and 
classroom activities.

CONFIDENTIALITY
Leadership is built on trust. I understand that I 
may become aware of information about students, 
families, or studio operations that is private. I will:
• Respect the privacy of students and families.
• Keep confidential information confidential.
• Direct questions to an instructor when 
appropriate.
• Never share private information with other 
students or on social media.
SOCIAL MEDIA & DIGITAL CITIZENSHIP
Everything I post reflects my personal character 
and the reputation of APA. I will:
• Use social media responsibly.
• Represent APA in a positive and respectful 
manner.
• Avoid posting content that could harm the 
reputation of another person or the studio.
• Obtain appropriate permission before sharing 
photos or videos taken during leadership 
activities.
PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION
Clear, respectful communication builds trust. I will:
• Respond to communication in a timely and 
respectful manner.
• Ask questions when I need clarification.
• Address questions or concerns directly with 
my instructor or Leadership Coordinator rather 
than discussing them with others.
• Speak about others with kindness, 
professionalism, and respect.
STUDENT SAFETY
Student safety is always our highest priority. I will:
• Follow all studio safety procedures.
• Ask for assistance whenever I am unsure.
• Immediately report injuries, safety concerns, or 
inappropriate behaviour to an instructor.
• Understand that the supervising instructor is 
always responsible for classroom decisions.
Professionalism is demonstrated in the small 
choices we make every day. At APA, those 
choices build trust, strengthen relationships, and 
shape the experience of every student and family.
COMMUNICATION 
& SCHEDULING
Great leaders communicate consistently. Staying 
informed helps you arrive prepared, support your 
team, and lead with confidence. To stay informed, 
I will:
• Check email and the leadership communication 
platform regularly.
• Read all studio communication thoroughly.
• Respond promptly when a reply is requested.
• Communicate scheduling conflicts as early as 
possible.
• Contact the Leadership Coordinator with 
questions or concerns.
• Keep my contact information up to date.
• Remember that parents will continue to receive 
important program communication throughout 
the season.
Clear communication helps our entire team 
succeed.
FEEDBACK & GROWTH
Leadership is a journey of continuous learning. 
Feedback helps you recognize your strengths, 
identify opportunities to grow, and continue 
developing as a leader. Throughout the season, I 
can expect:
• Classroom observations
• Ongoing coaching
• Goal setting
• Leadership reflection
• Recognition of progress
• Report cards, where applicable
Growth happens one step at a time. By remaining 
coachable, you’ll continue building confidence and 
leadership skills that last far beyond the studio.

ATTENDANCE  
& PARTICIPATION
Consistency builds trust. Students, instructors, and 
fellow leaders rely on you to be present, prepared, 
and engaged. As a Leaders in Training student, I 
am committed to:
• Attend scheduled leadership shifts, meetings, 
and training.
• Arrive on time and ready to lead.
• Communicate absences as early as possible.
• Arrange an approved substitute when required.
• Participate actively in leadership discussions, 
meetings, and monthly training.
• Remain dependable throughout the entire 
season.
Leadership isn’t measured by attendance alone, 
but commitment is where leadership begins.
DRESS CODE 
& PRESENTATION
Professionalism begins with presentation. Your 
appearance helps students and families recognize 
you as a trusted leader. When serving in my 
leadership role, I will:
• Wear the approved Leaders in Training uniform 
or other approved classroom attire.
• Ensure my clothing is clean, professional, and in 
good condition.
• Wear appropriate footwear.
• Keep my hair neatly secured away from my 
face.
• Avoid clothing, accessories, or logos that don’t 
align with APA’s dress code.
• Present myself professionally at all times.
Professional presentation reflects pride in yourself, 
your role, and our team.

CELEBRATING  
LEADERSHIP
Leadership is about more than reaching a 
destination. It’s about the growth, commitment, 
and positive impact you make along the way.
At APA, we believe character, consistency, and 
service are worth celebrating. Throughout the 
season, we recognize students who demonstrate 
our leadership values through their actions, 
attitude, and commitment to helping others.
LEADER OF THE MONTH
Each month, instructors nominate a student 
who has demonstrated exceptional leadership, 
professionalism, initiative, and service within the 
Leaders in Training Program.
Leader of the Month celebrates the everyday 
moments of leadership that strengthen our 
classrooms, encourage our students, and positively 
impact our studio community.
LEADER OF THE YEAR
At the conclusion of each season, one student 
from each leadership level is recognized as Leader 
of the Year.
Leader of the Year recipients are selected by the 
APA teaching team from the season’s Leader 
of the Month recipients. This award celebrates 
outstanding growth, consistency, leadership, and 
positive impact throughout the year.
LEVEL UP CEREMONY
Leadership is a journey, and every milestone 
deserves to be celebrated.
At the end of each season, students who are ready 
to advance to the next stage of the Leaders in 
Training Program are recognized during our Level 
Up Ceremony and presented with a certificate 
celebrating their achievement.
Progression is based on readiness, coachability, 
commitment, and continued growth, making each 
advancement a meaningful milestone in a student’s 
leadership journey.
Leadership isn’t something you’re given. It’s 
something you build, and every milestone along 
the way is worth celebrating.

Every classroom you assist in, every student you

simply to prepare great classroom assistants. It’s

comes next. The leadership skills below aren’t just 
valuable at APA—they’re skills you’ll carry with you 
for life.

PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT

include:
