Accelerated Program Overview
Dancers enrolled in our Accelerated Training Program (ATP) classes are committing to being held to a 
higher expectation; ATP Dancers are expected to have excellent attendance, etiquette, work ethic, and 
at-home practice habits. ATP Dancers will build a stronger technical foundation in graded technique 
classes and will work to develop artistry and performance skills.
Accelerated Program Eligibility
To be eligible for ATP classes, Dancers and their families must show interest and meet the skill level for 
the class. Some ATP classes may require attendance 2x per week or have a pre-requisite class. 
Please connect with us for exact class participation requirements and placements.
Accelerated Program Rules
Attendance
Good class attendance is required to remain in ATP classes. If attendance, work ethic, or skill progression 
fall below expectations, Dancers may have a pathway meeting or be moved to an Academy class if 
necessary.
Scheduling
• ATP classes must meet minimum enrollment in order to run.
• Training schedules are created with rest & recovery in mind; because we believe rest and 
recovery are essential for injury prevention, strength development, and long-term sustainability 
in training. Dancer schedules (including any Company Team Hours) will range from 3-5 days per 
week, depending on your Dancer’s commitment level.
• Training schedule hours will be between 4:00pm and 9:30pm - as Dancers age up through our 
program, their class times will begin later in the evening. 
• Have a problem with a class time? Connect with us and we will work together create a solution 
that works for you!

Throughout our season, we try to stagger the introduction of new content throughout the various 
disciplines in Accelerated Training Program. This means that when a Dancer joins an ATP class in August 
they may be joining a class that is well into the required work for their level and that there may be some 
repetitiveness before new content is introduced OR some catch-up.
Why do we do this? For Dancers who train in multiple disciplines, this helps reduce mental and physical 
burn out (because the introduction of new content is spread out over the year), and this also helps 
spread out payments for families who choose to examine in multiple disciplines!

Dancers who join the ATP later in their dance education:
• All placements in our Accelerated Training Program are based off of skill level. However, Dancers 
who are older generally progress through the levels faster as they are more physically and mentally 
developed. 
• It can take 2 to 3 years to adjust to the schedule, syllabus requirements, and style of the classes. Be 
patient - dance is a journey!!!  
Looking to progress faster?
If your Dancer is eager to progress faster, we can help! Connect with us to discuss options that are 
available to your Dancer.
Class Structure
ATP classes will comprise of various parts, including, but not limited to: Warm Up, Barre Work, Centre 
Floor Exercises, Across the Floor Exercises, Combinations, Flexibility and Strength Training, Mental and/
or Emotional Training, and more! This means you might see some unconventional exercises happening in 
your Dancer’s classroom if you take a peek through our viewing windows! 
Generally speaking, new content is taught to the entire group and as students progress more small 
group work and one-on-one time will be provided throughout class.
Dancers who are preparing for exams will be given the opportunity to perform required skills and 
combinations in small groups or individually, as required by each Governing Examination Body. For 
example; as Dancers prepare for Al Gilbert Tap Exams, Exam Candidates will dance individually (as 
required in their examination), while the rest of the class dances in pairs or small groups. Doing so allows 
Instructors to cover all of the required exam content, and ensure that Dancers who choose to examine 
are fully prepared for their examination while involving all students in the classroom. 
If you have any questions about the content or structure of your Dancer’s class, please contact your 
Dancer’s teacher. 
The Role of the ATP Family
The success of an ATP Dancer is built on partnership. ATP families support consistent attendance, 
encourage regular at-home practice, communicate proactively with instructors, and trust the long-term 
training plan. Most importantly, ATP families model resilience - understanding that growth includes 
challenge, correction, and perseverance. When dancers and families commit together, progress becomes 
powerful and sustainable.
Injury, Wellness & Return to Dance
Dancers are expected to communicate injuries early so we can prevent minor concerns from becoming 
larger setbacks. When an injury occurs, we have structured tools in place to support safe recovery and 
responsible return to dance.
Practitioner Assessment Form
If medical care is sought, a completed Practitioner Assessment Form is required so our Team clearly 
understands restrictions, modifications, and timelines for return. This ensures we are aligned with your 
healthcare provider and supporting long-term health.
Class Observation Forms
If a Dancer is unable to participate physically, they are still expected to attend class whenever possible. 
Observation keeps them mentally engaged, accountable, and connected to their training and their Team. 
Observation forms reinforce choreography retention, technical awareness, and correction application.
Injury recovery also builds mental resilience. Navigating setbacks with maturity, patience, and discipline 
is part of the growth process.

ATP Class Curriculum
Tap
Graded Levels: Graded Tap Levels will follow a combination of Al Gilbert and ADAPT Tap Syllabus.
Rhythm and Tricks: This class will focus on rhythm, trick steps, and different tap styles. 
Jazz
Graded Levels: Graded Jazz Levels will follow ADAPT Jazz Syllabus.
Jumps and Turns: This class will focus on higher level skills, and style.
Ballet
Graded Levels: Graded Ballet Levels will follow RAD Ballet Syllabus.
Acro
Graded Levels: Acro Levels will follow Acrobatic Arts Acro Syllabus - Where appropriate, multiple levels 
may be combined into one class. 
Aerials, Walkovers, and Handsprings: This class will focus on aerial, walkover, and handspring 
progressions and variations.
Hip Hop
Hip Hop classes will give Dancers the opportunity to learn and explore various styles of street dancing. 
This class helps Dancers work their levels, dynamics, and timing, which transfers to all other disciplines. 
Lyrical / Contemporary
Lyrical / Contemporary classes will give Dancers the opportunity to learn and explore the emotional side 
of dance. This class helps Dancers work their overall movement patterns and artistry, which improves 
performance and range of motion in other disciplines. Components of Modern dance will also be 
included.
