## A. Approved Q&A (safe to load)

### Dates & programs

**Q: When is the year-end dance recital?**  
**A:** The Year End Dance Recital is scheduled for June 4–5 at Bert Church LIVE Theatre. Exact show times and call times are not available yet. Dates may change depending on school calendars — for confirmation, contact the office at office@ambitionarts.com or 403-648-5287.

**Q: When is the year-end music or theatre recital?**  
**A:** The Year End Music/Theatre Recital is scheduled for June 18–19 at The Polaris Theatre. Exact show times and call times are not available yet. Dates may change depending on school calendars — for confirmation, contact the office at office@ambitionarts.com or 403-648-5287.

**Q: When is the last day of classes?**  
**A:** The last day of classes is June 19.

**Q: Which recital does my dancer go to?**  
**A:** Dance classes take part in the Year End Dance Recital on June 4–5 at Bert Church LIVE Theatre. Which specific showing your performer is in is not available yet and will be shared with recital information during Parent Participation Week (January 18–23). For help sooner, contact the office at office@ambitionarts.com or 403-648-5287.

**Q: Which recital does my music or theatre student go to?**  
**A:** Music and theatre take part in the Year End Music/Theatre Recital on June 18–19 at The Polaris Theatre. Specific showing details are not available yet and will be shared during Parent Participation Week (January 18–23). For help sooner, contact the office at office@ambitionarts.com or 403-648-5287.

**Q: Do sessional or summer programs perform in the year-end recital?**  
**A:** Sessional and summer programs do not take part in the year-end recital.

**Q: When do we get full recital information / the theme?**  
**A:** Recital information and the theme are released during Parent Participation Week, January 18–23. If you need support before then, contact the office at office@ambitionarts.com or 403-648-5287.

**Q: When is Photo Week?**  
**A:** Photo Week is May 10–15.

**Q: What happens in May before recital?**  
**A:** The studio is closed May 21–27. In-class dress rehearsals run May 28–June 3.

### Venues

**Q: Where is the dance recital / what is the dance venue?**  
**A:** The Year End Dance Recital is at Bert Church LIVE Theatre, 1000 East Lake Blvd NE, Airdrie, AB T4A 1P6.

**Q: Where is the music or theatre recital / what is the Polaris venue?**  
**A:** The Year End Music/Theatre Recital is at The Polaris Theatre, 261051 Wagon Wheel View #7, Rocky View County, AB T4A 0E2.

### Costumes

**Q: Are recital costumes included?**  
**A:** For dance and theatre students, recital costumes are included in monthly tuition. Costumes are handed over recital-ready — steamed, on a hanger, in a dry cleaner bag.

**Q: When is costume measuring?**  
**A:** Costume measuring week is November 1–7.

**Q: Can I get a refund on the costume after measuring?**  
**A:** After November 1, the costume charge is non-refundable, and remaining installments must still be completed.

**Q: How do I know when to pick up the costume?**  
**A:** You will be emailed when the costume is ready. Any costumes not picked up by June are donated.

### Tickets & photos

**Q: Do we get recital tickets included?**  
**A:** It depends on the program:
- Dance and theatre: tickets are an optional purchase — you choose how many for the Christmas show and the Year End Recital.
- Music students (Performer Package): 2 standard tickets to each show (Christmas and Year End) are included.
- Petite Boutique (ages 3+): 2 standard tickets to each show are included, plus a Memory Mate photo package.

**Q: How / where / when do we buy optional tickets?**  
**A:** Optional tickets are purchased through the theatre ticket pages:
- Dance (Bert Church): https://tickets.airdrie.ca/Events
- Music/Theatre (Polaris): https://www.showpass.com/s/events/calgary/?search_string=polaris
Ticket sale dates have not been set yet. When sales open, tickets go on sale at 12:00pm. For help, contact the office at office@ambitionarts.com or 403-648-5287.

**Q: Are photos included?**  
**A:** Photos are an optional purchase for everyone. Petite Boutique (ages 3+) includes a Memory Mate photo package.

### Hair & makeup

**Q: What hair and makeup should performers do for recital?**  
**A:** APA shares recital hair and makeup tutorials here:

- Hair tutorial: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pfc3HAgK5uU

### Cancellations & contact

**Q: Can we cancel during recital season?**  
**A:** No cancellations are accepted from March 1 through June 30 because of show preparations.

**Q: Who should I contact with recital questions?**  
**A:** Please contact the office at office@ambitionarts.com or 403-648-5287.
